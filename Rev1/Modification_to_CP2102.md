# Modifications to 6-pin CP2102 USB-Serial Adapter
This modification is required to enable CTS handshake for 6-pin CP2102 USB-serial adapter. The existing pin 1 is connect to DTR which needs to be cut and a short wire connects pin 1 to CTS.
